# 📊 Global Superstore Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Week 2 Data Analytics project with AnalystLab Africa Consulting.

The objective was to analyze Global Superstore sales data and develop an interactive dashboard that enables management to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, product performance, and shipping performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focused on answering key business questions:

1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Which products are the most profitable?
6. What trends can be observed over time?
7. What recommendations should management implement to improve business performance?

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides an interactive view of:

- Sales performance by region
- Sales and profit trends by year
- Sales by customer segment
- Profit by product
- Sales by category
- Top 5 countries by sales
- Sales and profit by ship mode
- Sub-category sales, quantity and profit margin

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard shows that Technology generated the highest category sales, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales, while the Central region recorded the highest regional sales among the displayed regions.

The United States was the leading country by sales, while Standard Class was the dominant shipping mode by sales volume.

The analysis also highlights differences in profitability across products and sub-categories, providing opportunities for management to focus on high-performing and high-margin products.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing regions and countries while investigating opportunities to improve weaker markets.

2. Prioritize profitable products and sub-categories rather than focusing solely on sales volume.

3. Develop targeted strategies for the Consumer segment, which represents the largest share of sales.

4. Review shipping performance and customer preferences to determine whether shipping modes are affecting profitability.

5. Monitor sub-category profit margins to identify products that generate strong revenue but relatively low profitability.
